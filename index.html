Frequently Asked Questions.

What is the total NOTCHLIVE supply?

Total supply is 11,111 NFTs, in one pool with no free/paid split. Free and paid mints both draw random unminted tokens from the same pool, not gallery order, and the web gallery stays at only the 40 sample works.

Why does the free mint have a fee?

The NFT itself is free (0 BNB). You only pay a 0.0001 BNB mint fee per mint, which goes to the owner's wallet as a creator fee.

How much is the paid mint?

0.001 BNB per NFT. The total sent is mint amount × 0.001 BNB, plus BNB Chain gas.

Can I mint without connecting a wallet manually?

Yes. Paste your wallet address in the AI Agent section — both prompts fill in automatically — then copy the prompt and send it to your AI agent with access to a BNB Chain wallet.

Can I mint with a Binance Agent ID?

Yes. If your wallet holds a Binance Agent ID, you can mint with it, in the manual flow and in the AI Agent flow.
